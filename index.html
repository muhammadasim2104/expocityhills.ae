About the Project

Eco-Luxury Living at the Heart of Expo City

Expo City Hills by Dubai South Properties introduces a new chapter of refined, sustainable living in one of Dubai's most visionary districts. Set at the strategic crossroads of Expo Road (E77) and Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road (E311), the development offers low-rise residences designed for those who value space, serenity, and connection.

Every element — from eco-friendly architecture and lush green areas to leisure pools and jogging tracks — reflects a commitment to wellness, community, and environmental responsibility. With retail shops integrated at ground level and advanced security throughout, Expo City Hills delivers a complete lifestyle within minutes of Expo City Dubai, world-class parks, schools, and golf courses.
